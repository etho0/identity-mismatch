identity-mismatch

Research tooling for authorized web and container security testing.

identity_oracle.html — reports where a context's claimed identity, recorded permission state, container bridge surface and platform policy disagree with each other.

stream_holder.html / stream_prober.html — cross-origin media-state experiment: one page holds a live camera or screen capture, the other probes what a second origin can learn about or take from it.

Passive by default; media tests are opt-in. Append ?srv=https://your-server/log to post the fingerprint to your own receiver.
